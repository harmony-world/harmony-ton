# HARMONY Privacy Policy

Version 5 of 12 November 2026

This policy explains what data HARMONY receives about you, why it is needed and whom we share it with. Terms are used as in the Terms of Use.

## 1. What data we receive

**From Telegram when you sign in:** Telegram ID, first name, last name, @username, profile photo link, language, whether you have Telegram Premium.

**From you:** the wallet address you connected; campaign texts and photos; a campaign author's reports on how the funds raised were spent; support requests; profile settings.

**From the TON blockchain:** only your activity on HARMONY — donations to campaigns, tips to the platform and withdrawals of funds raised. We do not collect the rest of your wallet's history. Blockchain records are public in any case.

**From the identity verification service:** the result and status of the check, and service data needed to prevent fraud. The documents themselves, their scans and photos stay with the verification service; we do not have them.

**Technical data:** app failure reports and service logs.

## 2. Why

- so that you can sign in, start a campaign, donate and withdraw funds;
- to moderate campaigns and prevent fraud;
- to calculate the trust score and donor status;
- to send notifications through the Telegram bot;
- to find and fix errors.

We do not sell your data and do not show ads.

## 3. What other users see

If your profile is public: name, @username, photo, trust score, donor status, campaigns and total donated. You can hide your profile in the settings. Published campaigns and their authors are visible to everyone.

## 4. Whom we share data with

- **Telegram** — to run the mini app and the bot;
- **an identity verification service** — identity verification;
- **a translation service** — translating campaign texts into other languages;
- **the TON blockchain and services that give access to it** — sending and checking transactions;
- **hosting and technical providers** — running the app.

Each receives only what it needs for its task. Each of these services has its own privacy policy.

## 5. How long we keep it

As long as you have an account and as long as it is needed to run the service, keep campaign records and resolve disputes. Technical logs are kept for a limited time.

## 6. Your rights

Through support in the app you can ask us to:

- show what data we hold about you;
- correct it;
- delete your account and data.

We may keep what is needed to prevent fraud (for example, records of a block and of identity verification). Blockchain records cannot be deleted — that is how a blockchain works.

## 7. Storage on your device

The app stores service data on your device: settings and app state, such as the chosen language. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

## 8. Changes to this policy

Every version has a date. You will need to accept a new version the next time you sign in.

## 9. Contact

The "Support" section in your profile in the app.
