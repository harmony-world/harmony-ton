# HARMONY Terms of Use

Version 5 of 12 November 2026

## 1. What these terms cover

HARMONY is a Telegram mini app for raising donations on the TON blockchain. You can start a fundraiser (a campaign) here or support someone else's.

These terms are between you and the HARMONY project team ("we"). HARMONY is not registered as a legal entity. It is a non-commercial project run by a team of people.

By tapping "I accept" you confirm that you have read these terms and the Privacy Policy and agree to them. If you do not agree, do not use the app.

## 2. Who may use HARMONY

- You are 18 or older, or have reached the age at which the laws of your country let you do this on your own.
- You use your own Telegram account and your own wallet.
- You have not been blocked in HARMONY before.

## 3. How the service works

- Every campaign gets its own smart contract on the TON blockchain. Donations go to that contract, not to us.
- We do not hold your funds and have no access to your wallet. Only you sign transactions, in your own wallet.
- The campaign author withdraws what was raised to their own wallet. The goal does not have to be reached: the author receives whatever was raised.
- HARMONY takes no fee out of donations: the author receives the whole donation.
- TON network fees are paid by whoever sends the transaction. If we ever introduce a fee of our own, we will publish a new version of these terms first.

## 3.1. Tips to the platform

- When donating — in the app or in the campaign card in the chat with the bot — you can add a tip to the platform of 0% to 15% on top of the donation. It is set to 1% by default. You choose the size of the tip and can remove it entirely.
- Tips are voluntary and do not affect the donation: the author receives the full donation amount, and the tip is paid on top of it.
- The campaign's smart contract sends the tip straight to the HARMONY wallet. Tips pay for running and developing the platform. The campaign author does not receive them.
- Tips are not refunded, including when a campaign is frozen and donors take their donations back.
- Dollar amounts are shown for reference at the rate at the time of the transaction. Crypto rates change, and the actual value may differ.

## 4. We are not a party to the fundraiser

HARMONY is a platform that connects campaign authors and donors. We do not run fundraisers, do not collect money in our own name and are not responsible for how an author spends it.

We review campaigns before publication: each one is approved by a human moderator. Moderation does not guarantee that a story is true or that the money will be spent as promised. The decision to donate is yours.

## 5. Donations

- A donation is voluntary and free of charge. You receive no goods, services or rights in return.
- A blockchain transaction is irreversible. We cannot cancel a donation or return it at your request.
- If moderation freezes a campaign, donors can take their donations back through the smart contract, without the tips to the platform. There is no other way to get money back.
- Your donations are visible to everyone on the blockchain. You can hide your profile in the app, but the blockchain record stays.

## 6. Campaign authors

By starting a campaign, you undertake to:

- tell the truth about the purpose of the fundraiser and about yourself;
- spend what is raised on the stated purpose;
- upload only photos and texts you have rights to, with the consent of the people shown;
- not raise money for anything prohibited by the laws of your country or the donors' countries, or listed in section 8.

If you raised 50 dollars or more, upload an expense report within 14 days of the withdrawal. An approved report raises your trust score; a missing or rejected one lowers it.

The author alone is responsible for taxes and any other obligations arising from the donations received.

## 7. Identity verification and trust score

Some actions require identity verification (KYC), carried out by an external verification service. Documents stay with it; we receive the result of the check.

Every user has a trust score. It depends on identity verification, expense reports and other signals, and other users can see it.

## 8. What is prohibited

- raising money for weapons, drugs, violence, extremism, gambling or any other illegal activity;
- sexual content, scenes of cruelty, hate speech;
- money laundering and sanctions evasion;
- attempts to break into the service or the smart contracts, automated mass actions;
- using other people's accounts or wallets.

## 9. Moderation

If you disagree with a decision, write to support in the app.
